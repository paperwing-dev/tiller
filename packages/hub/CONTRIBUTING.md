Tiller Coding Hub is free software under the MIT License.

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that:

- You have the right to submit the contribution.
- Your contribution is provided under the MIT License.
- Your contribution may be used, modified, redistributed, and included in Tiller Coding Hub as permitted by that license.

This is not a copyright assignment or a separate contributor license agreement.
You keep your copyright in your contribution.

Until beta, contributions are limited to bug fixes; feature requests are not
being accepted. Keep pull requests focused, explain the user-facing behavior
change, and include the implementation plan and the model and effort used for
planning and implementation. Open a discussion before proposing an
architectural change.
